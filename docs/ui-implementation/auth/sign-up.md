# Sign Up

## Purpose and Layout

Use the authentication shell. Collect Full name, Email and Password only unless an approved authentication decision changes the data. Show `Create your account`, concise supporting copy, full-width `Create account`, sign-in link, and quiet Terms and Privacy acknowledgement. Do not show identity-provider buttons.

## Behavior and States

Allow paste, password managers and password visibility. Explain the minimum password rule without an elaborate strength ceremony. Show field, duplicate-account, network, submitting and success states without account enumeration. Continue to verification or onboarding; do not configure teams here.

## Acceptance

Verify both themes, autofill, keyboard use, mobile controls, legal links and preserved invitation or destination context.
